About JustiFi

Bangladesh's modern legal-tech platform, built to make trusted legal help reachable for everyone.

Our Mission

JustiFi connects everyday people across Bangladesh with verified Advocates of the Supreme Court and District Bars, and digitizes the paperwork — affidavits, verifications, and consultations — that usually takes weeks of chamber visits down to a few clicks.
